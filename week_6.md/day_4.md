## Day 4 - Preparing Structured Output and Quality Control:
As I approached the end of the 6th week of my internship, I focused on finalizing the data transformation process. Today was about saving the parsed results into a structured format and rigorously verifying the accuracy of the automated system.

1. Practical Coding: Generating Structured Output (JSON)
I exported the output generated by Drain3—specifically recording the template ID and the extracted dynamic variables for each individual log line—and saved it cleanly into a JSON file. This structured dataset is critical, as it will serve as the foundational bedrock for the anomaly detection models I will be building in Week 7.

2. Quality Control: Manual Verification
To ensure the integrity of the automated parsing, I conducted a manual quality control check. I randomly selected 10 lines from the raw logs and meticulously compared them manually against their assigned templates. My goal was to verify if the template assigned by Drain3 truly and accurately represented the original log line. This verification step ensured that the structured data I am passing to the next phase is highly reliable.
