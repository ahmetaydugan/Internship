## Day 2 - Main Dashboard Cards and Visualizing Findings:
On the second day of the week, I focused on transforming my Streamlit application from a simple data table into a comprehensive analytical dashboard, designing the primary visual components to highlight key metrics.

1. Practical Coding: Building the Dashboard Cards
I developed the core visual elements of the UI. I implemented summary metric cards at the top of the dashboard to display the total number of anomalies at a glance. Below that, I integrated a template distribution graph and a time-series line chart to visualize the occurrence frequency trends dynamically.

2. Analytical Visualization: The Over-Clustering Finding
I specifically designed a section to visualize a critical finding from Week 6: the over-clustering issue. By graphing the difference between the initial 17 templates and the refined 14 templates side-by-side, I visually demonstrated how adjusting the Drain3 similarity threshold impacts the granularity of the parsed data. Seeing this theoretical tuning represented as a concrete visual chart made the data engineering process much easier to comprehend.
