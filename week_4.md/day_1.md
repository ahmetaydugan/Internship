## Day 1 - Words Turn into Numbers:
Starting a new week of my internship, I transitioned from image processing and numerical datasets into the world of Natural Language Processing (NLP). Today was all about understanding how machines comprehend human language by turning words into meaningful arrays of numbers.

1. Historical Concept: The Word2Vec Revolution
I began the day by exploring the 2013 Word2Vec breakthrough. Learning how words are represented in a vector space completely shifted my perspective. The famous equation "king - man + woman = queen" perfectly illustrates this. It laid the foundational theme for this week: words are no longer just static text strings, but mathematical vectors carrying deep semantic meaning.

2. Academic Block: The Illustrated Word2Vec
To solidify my theoretical understanding, I read Jay Alammar's highly acclaimed article, "The Illustrated Word2Vec." Visualizing the underlying mechanics of how these embeddings are generated helped me grasp the abstract concepts of vector spaces and semantic relationships much more clearly.

3. Practical Coding: Sentence Transformers and Cosine Similarity
Moving from theory to practice, I installed the sentence-transformers library. I converted several words and sentences into embeddings and used cosine similarity to test their mathematical closeness. Seeing the code successfully prove that "king" and "queen" are semantically close, while "king" and "apple" are far apart, was a profound realization of how machines actually "read" and measure context.
